Short Biography

Bao Zhao is a tenure-track professor and PhD supervisor at Beijing Institute of Technology in Zhuhai. He leads the Lab of Autonomous Structures and Systems (LASS). His research bridges piezoelectric energy conversion, nonlinear metamaterials, structural sensing, and self-powered intelligent systems.

Before joining Beijing Institute of Technology, he was an RGC Junior Research Fellow and a Distinguished Postdoctoral Fellow at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. He received his PhD in Structural Mechanics from ETH Zürich.

Research Interests

Our group is broadly interested in autonomous structures and systems. Current research topics include:

  • Integrated energy harvesting and control, including power electronics and electromechanical systems
  • Distributed intelligent sensing, including structural health monitoring and sensor technologies
  • Autonomous intelligent structures and systems, including self-powered AI and self-sensing systems
